Building a balanced Plate

Fuel your body with the right nutrients for optimal health.

Essential Food Groups

A healthy plate supports activities by providing lasting energy. Aim to include:

  • Vegetables: Aim for a variety of colors.
  • Proteins:Include lean meats, beans, or tofu.
  • Grains:Choose whole grains for more fibre.

Learn more about healthy eating here: HelpGuide.org

Energy-Boosting snack ideas

We should choose snacks which are healthy and provide us with a alot of energy to work, study and play, instead of processed junk like burger and fries.

Quick Pantry Picks:

  • Roasted Chickpeas (foe a salty crunch)
  • Apple slices with almond butter
  • Hummus and carrot sticks

Nutrition Tips for Students

Stay hydrated with enough water

Water helps regulate your temperature, transport nutrients and oxygen to cells, lubricate your joints, and keeps our brain sharp.

Eat The Rainbow

Aim for atleast 400 grams of fruits and vegetables daily. Different colors offer distinct antioxidents, vitamins and minerals.
