Mini Biography: Mahatma Gandhiji

Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi was an Indian lawyer, anti-colonial nationalist and political ethicist who employed nonviolent resistance to lead the successful campaign for India's independence from British rule .

Early Life

He was born on October 2, 1869, in the coastal town of Porbandar in Gujarat.

He returned to India in 1891 to begin his legal career, shortly before taking a life-changing case in South Africa that sparked his journey into civil rights activism.

Important Achievement

He played a major role in the life-long struggle for India's freedom. On August 15th, 1947, his goal of freedom for his country became a reality.

He is remembered as the Father of the Nation because he pioneered and practiced the principle of Satyagraha-resistance to tyranny through mass nonviolent civil disobedience.

One value that made him special was Compassion and Kindness.

Correction Corner

He only worked as a politician. He was also a social activist, writer and lawyer.
